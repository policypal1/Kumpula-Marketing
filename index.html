FAQ

Before we launch.

Is ad spend included in your management fee?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Google through your Google Ads account. Management and build costs are separate.

Do you guarantee a certain number of leads?

No. Results depend on the market, budget, competition, offer, website, and sales follow-up. I focus on measurable campaigns and improving performance from real data.

Do I need a new website before running ads?

Not always. If your current site can convert traffic, we can use it. If it is the bottleneck, I can build a focused landing page or replace the site.

What businesses do you work with?

I focus on local service businesses where a call or quote request can turn into a meaningful job, including home services, automotive, remodeling, trades, and similar businesses.
